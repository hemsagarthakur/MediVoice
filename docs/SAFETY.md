# Medical Safety Boundaries

## What MediVoice does

MediVoice explains text already visible on a prescription or medicine label. It extracts fields, presents one understandable review, preserves missing information, localizes a deterministic plain-language explanation, and reads that explanation aloud.

## What it never does

MediVoice does not diagnose, prescribe, recommend treatment, validate a prescription's medical correctness, infer missing instructions, change dosage, detect all interactions, or generate medicine purpose, side effects, or warnings from model memory.

## Extraction and uncertainty

- Groq receives OCR text as untrusted delimited data in live mode and must return null for absent fields.
- The provider may explicitly return no medicines. Zero substantive medicines produces `no_medicine_detected`; MediVoice never creates a placeholder or guessed medicine to satisfy the public success schema.
- Schema validation is necessary but does not prove semantic correctness.
- A missing medicine name blocks confirmation.
- Model-marked uncertain information is highlighted and requires explicit acknowledgement or a verified correction.
- Missing or uncertain critical fields produce Low confidence and require review. Optional missing information may produce Medium confidence without forcing confirmation.
- Confidence is categorical and deterministic. It is not model confidence and no unsupported percentage is displayed.
- A missing value remains visibly `Not detected` and is stated as missing in the explanation. A missing non-name field does not create a second field-by-field confirmation step unless the extractor marked it uncertain.
- A per-dose count of five or more tablets, capsules, or puffs triggers an additional deterministic verification warning and acknowledgement; it is not presented as a medical judgment.
- The original OCR and original extraction remain available only during the active review session.
- Invalid, empty, or unusable extraction stops the flow with retry/rescan guidance.

## Medicine-strip policy

Packaging often lacks patient-specific directions. The extractor must not treat product strength or phrases such as “dosage as directed by physician” as patient dosage. The confirmation and result screens always remind the user to check the prescription or pharmacist when dosage is unavailable.

## Translation and speech

Only a confirmed deterministic English explanation may be translated. Medicine names, strengths, and numeric facts are protected from translation and restored exactly. Speech uses only the selected successful localized explanation. Translation or TTS failure never modifies confirmed fields and never causes a generated fallback instruction.

## Privacy and external processing

No patient identity is required. Images stay on the device. In live mode, OCR text is sent to the backend and Groq. In explicit demo mode, it is sent only to the mock backend and must match synthetic fixtures. MediVoice does not persist or log it in either mode. Tests use synthetic or anonymized data.

Demo mode never activates as a fallback for a failed live request, never invents data for unknown text, and cannot bypass review, acknowledgement, strip safeguards, deterministic summary generation, translation gating, or speech gating.

## Emergencies and trusted data

The app provides no emergency triage. Users must contact qualified clinicians or emergency services for urgent concerns.

General medicine information is sourced separately from RxNorm and official drug-label data (DailyMed preferred, openFDA fallback). It is never generated by Groq and is never presented as the doctor's reason for prescribing. Only strongly matched, confirmed medicine identities may receive reference facts; uncertain, ambiguous, unmatched, or unavailable lookups show no guessed facts. Side effects are described as common only when the label explicitly supports that wording. Reference information is general, not personalized medical advice, and its failure cannot block scanned instructions.

## Safety tests

Tests must prove that missing dose/duration remain null, strip scans do not create dosage, confident results do not require field-by-field confirmation, uncertain and suspicious fields require acknowledgement, plain-language explanations add no unsupported medical facts, protected translation facts remain exact, TTS receives only the localized explanation, uncertain medicine identities receive no guessed reference facts, reference failure is non-blocking, malformed AI output fails closed, and unsafe prompt content cannot alter the schema or instructions.
